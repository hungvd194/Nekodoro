# Nekodoro concept v1

Tool: built-in imagegen. Intent: new UI-mockup image, design proposal, not production UI or extracted assets.

Prompt:

> Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished wide landscape UI/UX concept presentation for an original Windows Pomodoro app called "Nekodoro", combining cats and Pomodoro with an Irasutoya-inspired Japanese friendly public-information illustration aesthetic: softly irregular hand-painted flat shapes, faint paper-grain in the illustrations, tiny dark dot eyes, modest warm humorous expressions, friendly slightly clumsy cream-and-gray cat with a tiny tomato accessory. Original cat character, no existing copied mascot. This is a proposed actual desktop app interface, not a landing page. Show two beautifully composed app windows side-by-side on a quiet warm off-white background, generous separation, crisp readable native-app controls with 12px rounded buttons, no glass/glows, no gradients, no drop-shadow-heavy cards. Palette: paper ivory #F7F5EE, white panels, warm charcoal #403D37 text, muted tomato red #BF6559 primary, sage #7F9276, pale blue #97B4BE. Left window is compact main timer: exact brand heading "Nekodoro", subline "Một nhịp nhỏ, một ngày nhẹ hơn.", a charming hand-painted cat sitting next to a small tomato timer, main large time "25:00", state "Tập trung", one primary button "Bắt đầu", secondary controls "Bỏ qua" and "Cài đặt", small 4 empty paw-shaped cycle dots with label "Chu kỳ 0/4". Right window is settings, heading "Cài đặt", small simple navigation tabs "Nhịp", "Thanh", "Màu", "Ứng dụng". Show the Thanh view with a small screen preview and a thin sage progress line at its top edge, obvious four screen-edge options, sliders for length and thickness; below show three neat swatch selectors labeled "Tập trung", "Nghỉ ngắn", "Nghỉ dài". Footer actions exact "Hủy" and "Lưu thay đổi". Small cat peeking quietly near the settings heading, not decorating every control. Beneath windows include a small tray icon detail of a cat face with tomato-red cheeks and a tiny four-pose original cat expression lineup: reading, tea break, asleep, waving. Small caption "Nekodoro — UI/UX concept" at bottom. UI words must be clean accurate Vietnamese where provided, otherwise keep text sparse. High-quality product design board; interface typography modern clear humanist sans with Vietnamese accents, cat drawings have warm handmade Irasutoya-like clipart appeal, illustrations are flat slightly textured not 3D, not anime, not pixel art. Main screen must feel calm, cute and genuinely usable. Do not include website navigation, marketing CTAs, fake dashboards, sparkles, excessive hearts or extraneous charts.
